Project Lichen is a framework for reconnecting living systems, energy systems, human coordination, land use, technology and governance into one coherent design. We already have many pieces in place and working; the most active part of this, being The Fabaceae Food Forest; we introduced this concept [here](https://foodscaping.substack.com/p/the-fabaceae-food-forest-concept).

# About Us

---

Project Lichen views ecosystems as interconnected living systems and...

It all started in 2011 in Hermosa Beach, California and nearby El Segundo, both are part of Greater LA in Southern California.  Here are highlights of how all emerged and evolved [and here is a pictorial record of most of this](https://incredibleedibleeugene.wordpress.com/blog/)   

**2009** Mike Brunt (me) met Alexandra Bwye who will recur here.

**2010** Alexandra and Mike travel Australia and New Zealand partly because of Mike's tech work in Java and partly to research regenerative living methodologies.

**2011** Mike moved to Waiheke Island, New Zealand to dig deeper into Biodynamics, Permaculture and off-grid related living.

**2012** Alexandra and Mike study to become Permaculture Certified Designers with Alexandra's apartment complex as the practical working example.

**2013** Alexandra and Mike pass the PDC course and tests and Become Permaculture Certified Designers.

**2014** Alexandra and Family and Mike move to Eugene, Oregon.  They begin land work and construction at Million Daisy Farm in Eugene, aided by Jeff Schaid as a Master Joiner.

**2015** Alexandra creates the Eugene Permaculture Meetup group and a new permaculture project starts at Burp-Hollow Farm in Cheshire, Oregon.  Gypsy-style chicken coop and Polytunnel buit.

**2016** Alexandra, Jeff & Mike start major projects at Burp Hollow Farm involving 3/4 acre fenced Food Forest and 9,500 gallon Water Capture & Storage constructed.

**2017** Mike moves into Eugene city and into a permaculture community of 5 dwellings.  This started a Fava Bean journey and the Eugene Seed Exchange build out.

**2018** The formal start of the Eugene Seed Exchange begins in earnest is a long kitchen drawer.

**2019** Mike and Jeff move into a .8 acre lawn enclosed property in Eugene and begin the Golden Urban Permaculture Institute (GUPI).

**2020** GUPI becomes the main focal growing area for the Reality Kitchen community projects supporting adult-learning. COVID hits causing the GUPI project to wind-down.  Annie Mims Community Gardens North are created on Maxwell Rd in Eugene.

**2021** Afterthe impacts of COVID bite there are a series of projects in and around Eugene and Veneta focussed on Willow planting including Oregon Country Fair grounds.

**2022** A new 3 year urban permaculture project commences in Eugene and the focal point becomes the Eugene Seed Exchange consolidation.  Jeff builds out a tiny-home for Mike.

**2023** Regenerate Cascadia is created and Mike is asked to steward the Cascadia Seed Guild, using the Eugene Seed Exchange as the core, which has now grown to 1,276 varieties of seeds.

**2024** Mike assumes sterwardship of the Agrarian Sharing Network (ASN) seed events.

**2025** Mike moves out to Biome Farm in Veneta to co-manage garden projects.

**2026** Those Fava's again - 2025-26 is a record harvest year with over 25,000 Fava Beans grown and harvested.  Project Lichen is created and the E-Cropolis game possibility presents itself.
