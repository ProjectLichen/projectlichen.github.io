# Origins & Evolution

> **Project Lichen did not begin as a theoretical framework.**

The name Project Lichen arrived in 2026, but the ideas behind it emerged through many years of practical experiments with food, seed, soil, water, land, community and technology.

There was no original master plan.

One project led to another.

Questions encountered in one place reappeared somewhere else.

Seed saving led toward questions of reproduction and resilience.

Food growing led toward soil, water, biodiversity and pollinators.

Community projects raised questions about coordination and shared knowledge.

Technology raised questions about distance, dependency and where capability resides.

Gradually, relationships that had once appeared to belong to separate subjects began to look like parts of the same inquiry.

Project Lichen became a way of connecting them.

This chronology records some of that path.

---

## Before Project Lichen

The following history predates the name.

It matters because Project Lichen grew from practice before it became a framework.

It all started in 2009 in Hermosa Beach, California and nearby El Segundo, both are part of Greater LA in Southern California.  Here are highlights of how all emerged and evolved [and here is a pictorial record of most of this](https://incredibleedibleeugene.wordpress.com/blog/)   

**2009** Mike Brunt (me) met Alexandra Bwye who will recur here.

**2010** Alexandra and Mike travel Australia and New Zealand partly because of Mike's tech work in Java and partly to research regenerative living methodologies.
 *As a note point, Permaculture originated from Bill Mollison and David Holmgren in Australia.*

**2011** Mike moved to Waiheke Island, New Zealand to dig deeper into Biodynamics, Permaculture and off-grid related living. *During Mike's time on unreticulated (no mains water supply) Waiheke Island there was a significant drought which was mitigated by Waiheke's well developed rain-water capture and storage capabilities. A key learning point.*

**2012** Alexandra and Mike study to become Permaculture Certified Designers with Alexandra's apartment complex, in Greater LA, as the practical working example. *This was the first main exposure to Alexandra and Mike of the key importance of permaculture to concentrated urban environments such as towns and cities.*

**2013** Alexandra and Mike pass the PDC course and tests and Become Permaculture Certified Designers. *From the origins of our permaculture journey, Alexandra and I determined that we would spread the benefits of permaculture far and wide.*

**2014** Alexandra and Family and Mike move to Eugene, Oregon.  They begin land work and construction at Million Daisy Farm in Eugene, aided by Jeff Schaid as a Master Joiner. *After our urban work in El Segundo . Los Angeles we now had the opportunity to work in full practicalities on larger projects, as follows.* 

**2015** Alexandra creates the Eugene Permaculture Meetup group and a new permaculture project starts at Burp-Hollow Farm in Cheshire, Oregon.  Gypsy-style chicken coop and Polytunnel build.  

**2016** Alexandra, Jeff & Mike start major projects at Burp Hollow Farm involving 3/4 acre fenced Food Forest and 9,500 gallon Water Capture & Storage constructed.

**2017** Mike moves into Eugene city and into a permaculture community of 5 dwellings.  This started a Fava Bean journey and the Eugene Seed Exchange build out. *Back into urban environments, most food is eaten in towns and cities and urban permaculture is a key benefit system in this regard.*

**2018** The formal start of the Eugene Seed Exchange begins in earnest is a long kitchen drawer. *Seeds (and spores) are at the base of most terrestrial food systems and also water-focussed fish farming.*

**2019** Mike and Jeff move into a .8 acre lawn enclosed property in Eugene and begin the Golden Urban Permaculture Institute (GUPI). *At the outset, this property looked like a small park and eventually became the largest evolving urban permaculture project we had designed and worked on.*

**2020** GUPI becomes the main focal growing area for the Reality Kitchen community projects supporting adult-learning. COVID hits causing the GUPI project to wind-down.  Annie Mims Community Gardens North are created on Maxwell Rd in Eugene. *Important note, although the evolution of the GUPI project came to to a stop, the work already done continues growing, literally, a pivotal part being the Food Forest we planted with 21 fruit trees and 35 berry bushes, not to mention ongoing Fava Bean growings, twice yearly.*

**2021** After the impacts of COVID bite there are a series of projects in and around Eugene and Veneta focussed on Willow planting including Oregon Country Fair grounds. *The Oregon Country Fair project involved the planting of over 10,000 Willow seedlings. A project for the years*

**2022** A new 3 year urban permaculture project commences in Eugene and the focal point becomes the Eugene Seed Exchange consolidation.  Jeff builds out a tiny-home for Mike.

**2023** Regenerate Cascadia is created and Mike is asked to steward the Cascadia Seed Guild, using the Eugene Seed Exchange as the core, which had now grown to 1,276 varieties of seeds.

**2024** Mike assumes sterwardship of the Agrarian Sharing Network (ASN) seed events. *ASN is a tremendously important movement "The Agrarian Sharing Network (ASN) is a decentralized, collaborative network, focused on distributing plant diversity to friends and neighbors and creating a shareable reproducible model for others to implement. It’s as much about the plants as it is about the people".*

**2025** Mike moves out to Biome Farm in Veneta to co-manage garden projects. *Mike had created an article named "[The Incredible Edible Manifesto](https://foodscaping.substack.com/p/the-incredible-edible-manifesto)" which uncannily mirrors the physical aspects of Biome Farm.*

**2026** Those Fava's again - 2025-26 is a record harvest year with over 25,000 Fava Beans grown and harvested and [The Fabaceae Food Forest concept emerges.](https://foodscaping.substack.com/p/the-fabaceae-food-forest-concept)  [Project Lichen](https://foodscaping.substack.com/p/project-lichen) is created and the E-Cropolis game possibility presents itself.
